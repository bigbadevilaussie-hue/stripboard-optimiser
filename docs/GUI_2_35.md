# GUI 2.35 preparation and pass outcomes

The startup path no longer invokes the 2.25 qualification sampling procedure.
It uses the existing CPU allocation policy and one native --speedprobe 100
decoder invocation, as in the 2.08–2.24 decoder-only workflow. The 100 checks
are not an electrical certification or a full search. A matching completed
campaign may supply budget advice; otherwise 10k is a default, not a measured
quality recommendation. The historical sampling implementation is retained for
research but is not called by the GUI. No campaign runs until GO.

The Speed probe checklist row reflects CPU allocation, and JSON audit reflects
successful native decoder calibration. The board canvas clears after lock and
routing-rule confirmation. Saved board SVGs remain available on explicit preview.

A completed campaign with no qualifying boards is a failed pass in the GUI.
Its seed count remains completed, and its manifest remains COMPLETED for
resume/overwrite bookkeeping. The popup reports applied locks and the most
frequent CSV rejection reasons without attributing failure to any one part
without evidence.

Qt dialogs use the shared title bar app icon and centre on the main window,
clamped to the available screen. Dimension entries are compact and allow
1–999. These UI changes do not alter native solver limits or certify that every
large board is supported. No solver or rendering changes.
